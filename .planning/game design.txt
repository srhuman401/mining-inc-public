'Mining Game' (temporary title) is a mix between idle and management.

Title: "Mining INC.", final title.

Generic Plot:
	You arrive on an island and seek your hopes and dreams of
	running a massive mining corporation. These are your humble beginnings.

Gameplay:
	Player Involvement:
	Mine ores by clicking on them will damage then depending
	on your current pickaxe (can be upgraded via base upgrades)
	Place stations / items to expand, items are not player interactive.
	They solely add values;
	 for example: more furnaces = higher max smelting capacity.
	
	Players navigate ui menus to execute tasks like:
		Smelting,
		Base Upgrading,
		Researching,
		Placement,
		Selling,
	But can eventually employ workers to execute most tasks for them, like smelting and selling.
	
	Base Upgrading:
		Base Upgrading (or just, Upgrading) follows a linear path, it gradually increases in difficulty to upgrade
		by expanding its wide varity of requirements of resources as the player upgrades.
		
		Upgrading shows a visual difference in the players 'base' / company base, 
		with it visually starting as a tent, eventually evolving into a cabin or more.
		
		Base upgrading will not just be visual however, it can:
			- Unlock items(to craft)
			- Expand play area (in width)
			- And more.
		Base upgrading can only be done manually.

	Researching:
		Researching is, similar to base upgrading, a linear path. It is not a skill tree.
		It also gradually increases in difficulty to purchase as research levels up.	

		Researching allows you to discover the secrets of the island, it does not visually change the appearance of
		the company base or itself. And must be placed (only unlocked at Level 2 or 3)
		
		Typically it will cost more to research and take more time to complete (while base upgrades
		are almost instant), 		

		It can unlock:
			- Expansion of play area (in height)
			- More items(to craft, may be more powerful then base upgrade ones)
	Notes: 
		Base Upgrading unlocks width to represent the expansion of your company,
		Research unlocks depth to represent the exploration of the islands secrets.
		
		Both must be used together to truly expand your rule across the island, 	
		width will allow you to get more quantity (further = more ORES)
		but depth allows for more quality (deeper = better ORES)
	
	Gameplay Loop:
		Until you unlock drills and other automations (workers):
			Mine / Chop,
			Manage (Craft, Upgrade, Reserach, Smelt, Sell)
		Generically:
			Mine ores -> resources,
			smelt or refine ores (depends on type) -> sellables
			sell them -> money

	Side Features:
		Trees: spawn across the map, they can be mined manually or a worker can be employed to do it
		they are an essential resource for crafting many things. They regenerate.
